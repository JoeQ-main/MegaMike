Click anywhere or press a key to power up the audio engine.

Play with the mouse, the computer keyboard (Z and Q rows) or a MIDI keyboard.

Playing

  • Mouse: click and drag across the keys. Drag the wheels. Drag knobs up and down (hold Shift for fine control), scroll over them, double-click to reset.
  • Computer keyboard: lower row Z S X D C V G B H N J M , L . ; / starts at C3, upper row Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U I 9 O 0 P starts at C4. [ and ] shift octaves. Esc is all notes off.
  • MIDI: any connected input plays notes; CC1 is the mod wheel, pitch bend is the pitch wheel.

MegaMike behaviour

  • Monophonic, lowest-note priority, single trigger: contours only restart when no key was held.
  • The Decay rocker under the wheels gives the contours a release equal to their decay time.
  • External Input feeds the main output back into the mixer for the classic overdrive; the lamp shows the overload.
  • Emphasis above about 7 makes the filter sing on its own. Osc. 3 with range LO and keyboard control off is the LFO for the mod wheel.

Sequencer

  • Eight steps, each an eighth note at the Tempo. A step's knob sets its pitch above the root in semitones; its rocker turns the step into a rest. Gate sets how long each note is held.
  • While it runs, pressing any key moves the root of the sequence to that key. The pattern is remembered between sessions.
